Personal Injury

Accidents come in a variety of forms—automobile accidents, workplace injuries, or slips and falls.

Whatever the cause, we understand that personal injury accidents can be life changing and leave you in pain and financially stressed.

You don't have to go to one of the big injury attorneys to get the best representation. At large injury firms, you are just one case that will be passed from associate to associate. At Jones, Green, & Tackett Law, PLLC, we partner with a Nashville-based personal injury attorney to ensure that you will receive the best possible up-town service with the ease of small-town convenience. You will work personally with a dedicated attorney who will walk you through each step of the process and ensure that you receive the best settlement possible in a timely fashion.

So if you have been injured due to the negligence of another person, you may be entitled to your medical expenses, lost wages, pain and suffering, and your loss of the enjoyment of life. Contact us today to speak with one of our attorneys about your case.
