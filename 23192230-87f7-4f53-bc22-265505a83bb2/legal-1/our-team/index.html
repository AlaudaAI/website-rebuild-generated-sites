OUR PHILOSOPHY

Our philosophy at Jones, Green, & Tackett Law, PLLC, is to personally walk you through every step of your case—whether you are going through extensive litigation or simply executing documents. We understand that you are going through a stressful, unfamiliar experience that can be confusing and overwhelming. That is why we view the attorney-client relationship as a partnership and believe that communication is the foundation to a successful case strategy. From start to finish, you will have a legal team by your side and your attorney will be by your side to answer your questions and look after your best interests.

At Jones, Green, & Tackett Law, PLLC, our three attorneys have a total of over 28 years of collective legal experience in Robertson, Montgomery, Sumner, Davidson, and Cheatham counties and offer a wide variety of practice areas to best serve your legal needs.
