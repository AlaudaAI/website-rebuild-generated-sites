Appeals

Appeals

Representation for parties seeking to appeal a verdict or responding to an appeal filed by an opponent, including case strategy, brief drafting, and oral arguments before the Court.

After the Judge or jury has reached a verdict in your case, the losing party typically has the right to appeal the verdict and have the decision reviewed by a higher court.

There are a variety of reasons why an appeal may be appropriate in your case or why your opponent may have filed an appeal against you.

If you are considering filing an appeal or if your case has been appealed by your opponent, contact us for a free consultation with one of our attorneys.

At Jones, Green, & Tackett Law, PLLC, we make it a priority to keep you involved in your case and aware of each step along the way. From deciding the case strategy and drafting the brief to the oral arguments before the Court, our attorneys will guide you and advocate for your desired outcome.
