Criminal Law

Facing criminal charges is serious business and can be overwhelming to most people. If you do not have adequate representation, the consequences of a criminal conviction can range from losing your license or job to facing jail time.

This is why we take your case seriously and provide the best representation possible for each our clients. Working with Jones, Green, & Tackett Law, PLLC, you will receive discreet, zealous representation to reach the best possible conclusion for your case.

Contact us today to discuss your situation in more detail. We provide representation for all criminal charges, including the following:

  • Domestic Violence
  • Driving Under the Influence
  • Assault
  • Drug Offenses
  • Robbery
  • Burglary
  • Theft
  • Weapons Charge
  • Felony Charges
  • Misdemeanor Charges
