Mediation

A process where the parties in a court case attempt to settle their disputes with the assistance of a trained, impartial mediating attorney, offering a less expensive, confidential, and party-controlled alternative to litigation.

Whether you are considering mediation as an alternative to costly and stressful litigation or whether you are required to attend mediation by the Court, our attorneys are here to help you through the mediation process.

Mediation is a process where the parties in a court case attempt to settle their disputes with the assistance of a trained, impartial mediating attorney. This mediator goes back and forth between the parties in an attempt to help them find a solution that all parties can agree to.

Mediation can be incredibly beneficial to clients for a variety of reasons, which include that mediation is generally less expensive than litigation, that mediation is a confidential and private process as opposed to the more public nature of court hearings, and that mediation gives all parties a greater degree of control in the ultimate outcome.

Rule 31 mediation is based on principles of communication and negotiation that emphasize the needs and interests of the parties. Since the decision-making authority rests with the parties, mediation is a great way to resolve a dispute without the need for litigation.
