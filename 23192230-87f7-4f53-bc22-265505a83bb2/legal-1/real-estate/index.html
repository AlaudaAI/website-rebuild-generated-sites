Real Estate

When it comes to transferring ownership of real estate, Jones, Green, & Tackett Law, PLLC can help! Our firm handles real estate matters including Real Estate Closings, Quit Claim Deeds, Affidavits of Heirship, and Muniments of Title.

Real Estate Closings

A closing attorney is responsible for organizing, carrying out, and finalizing real estate closings. The real estate closing is an event that brings parties of a real estate transaction to a single location. During the closing, all relevant legal documents are signed, and the established payment is transferred from the buyer to the property owner. The buyer leaves the closing with the rights to the property. Whether you are buying or selling real estate, it is important to have an attorney for your real estate closing. A real estate attorney ensures that everyone understands the complex legal process. Contact us today to discuss your closing needs.

Transferring Title

Sometimes you will need help transferring title to real estate, even if you aren't using a real estate agent or needing a traditional closing. Whether you are simply giving someone property and need a Quit Claim Deed or whether you need help transferring title to property after the passing of a loved one, we can help.
