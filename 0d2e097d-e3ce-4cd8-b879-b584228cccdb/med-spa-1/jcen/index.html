Never stop learning

At Face Card Clinics, we believe that growth is rooted in knowledge. Our commitment to excellence goes beyond beauty—it's about staying at the forefront of advanced aesthetic techniques through continuous education.

Our team regularly participates in hands-on training, masterclasses, and certification programs focused on the latest injection techniques, safety protocols, and artistry in aesthetics. We prioritize client safety, natural results, and cutting-edge practices, ensuring every injector is confident, competent, and current. Joanna Zhu MSN, RN

CEO & Founder of Face Card Clinics 💉

Originally from California, Joanna earned her Bachelor's in public health from San Diego State University, followed by a Bachelor's of Nursing and Master's of Nursing from the University of Hawaii at Mānoa. She has five years of RN experience and one year of specialized training in aesthetics.

Dedicated to making beauty accessible in Oahu & Texas, Face Card Clinics provides high-quality, affordable treatments, including Botox, fillers, microneedling, PRP, laser hair removal, fat dissolvers, chemical peels, and more.
