Criminal Law

Representation of clients in state criminal courts in Sumner County and throughout Middle Tennessee.

Assault

Defense for clients accused of violent crimes such as domestic violence, assault, aggravated assault, sexual assault/rape, or battery, including cases involving sex offender registration. Cases handled range from simple assault to aggravated assault or assault with a deadly weapon.

Drug Crimes

Defense for all levels of drug charges, from simple marijuana possession or other drug possession to drug trafficking or drug manufacturing, charged as either misdemeanor or felony.

DUI

Defense for cases involving DUI/drunk driving, underage drinking, or driving under the influence of drugs.

Theft

Aggressive defense for misdemeanor or felony theft-related charges, from shoplifting to burglary or armed robbery.

Traffic Offenses

Challenges to speeding tickets and other traffic tickets involving reckless driving, driving under suspension, and more.

White Collar Crimes

Defense in white collar crime cases including embezzlement, accounting fraud, bank fraud, computer fraud, conspiracy, corporate fraud, mail fraud/wire fraud, money laundering, marketing fraud, and public deception.

Juvenile Crimes

Representation for juveniles in cases involving shoplifting, drug possession, underage drinking, assault, weapons, and other misdemeanors or felonies, pursuing sentencing alternatives that emphasize rehabilitation.
