Welcome

Hello, I’m Thomas Jay Martin, JR. Attorney At Law and I provide legal counsel in Tennessee. Thomas Jay Martin, JR. Attorney At Law was founded in 1991 and I currently serve the Sumner County area. I’ve been providing legal counsel for over 20yearsand I’m a member of the Tennessee bar association. I’m a former Tennessee administrative law judge and I was appointed Special Judge for Sumner County General Sessions, Circuit Court, and Juvenile Court. My legal services include injuries, workers compensation, misdemeanors, felonies, social security, family law and much more.
