About

Legal counsel for the Sumner County area

At Thomas Jay Martin, JR. Attorney At Law, I provide many legal services and legal counsel for the Sumner County area. Services offered are probate and wills, family law, driving offences, juvenile law, social security, felonies, misdemeanors, workers compensation and injuries. For over 20 years I’ve provided my legal services. I also provide my clients a free initial consultation. My business hours are Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. To receive legal services or to request your free consultation, feel free to visit my contact page.
