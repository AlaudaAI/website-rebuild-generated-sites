About Hong Kong Massage Studio

We are a dedicated team of licensed massage therapists committed to helping you relieve stress, manage pain, and restore your energy. Located conveniently in Pflugerville, we provide a clean, safe, and tranquil environment where your wellness is our only priority.

Whether you need deep work to release chronic muscle tension or a gentle session to unwind after a long week, we tailor every appointment to your specific needs.

We are specialized in...
001 Deep Tissue Massage

Targets inner muscle layers to release chronic tension. Using slower strokes and firm, direct pressure, this technique breaks down stiffness and painful knots. Ideal for relieving specific issues like stiff necks or lower back pain.

002 Traditional Thai Massage

An active therapy often described as 'assisted yoga.' Performed while fully clothed, the therapist uses rhythmic compression and gentle stretching to improve flexibility. Leaves you feeling energized and aligned without the use of oils or lotions.

003 Couples Massage

Couples Massage lets you share a relaxing experience side-by-side with a partner, friend, or family member. Two therapists work simultaneously in a private room, tailoring each person's massage to their specific needs. It is the perfect way to bond while you unwind together.

004 Cupping Therapy

Uses special cups to create suction on the skin, increasing blood flow to relieve deep tension and promote repair. Excellent for reducing pain and stiffness. Temporary circular marks may appear as a sign of released stagnation and natural healing.

005 Four Hands Massage

Four Hands Massage is a luxurious experience where two therapists work simultaneously with synchronized movements. The harmony of four hands helps your mind completely let go, offering a deeper level of relaxation than a standard massage. It is designed to disconnect you from stress and provide double the relief.
