About the Firm

Every legal strategy is custom tailored to our client's needs.

Walwyn & Walwyn has built a strong practice based upon determination, attention to detail, and a reputation forwinning. Our firm is very proud of winning cases where previous attorneys have given up.

You or a loved one should not have to suffer and pay for another persons negligence. Many times accidents will present long term negative effects that don't present right away. The lawyers at Walwyn & Walwyn understand your rights and how insurance companies will try to pressue your to settle for sums that won't cover your short or long term bills. Don't let them bully you into make a quick decision. Our attorneys will stand up and fight for your rights. We will push to get the settlement you deserve so you are not left with a heavy financial load to carry due to someone else's lack of concern or attention.

If you have suffered the death of a loved one caused by an accident, or if you have been badly injured, wrongly accused, or simply in need of courtroom lawyers willing to fight for you, Walwyn & Walwyn stands ready to help. Put us to work for you today.

What We Do

Workplace Injuries / Worker's Compensation

Tennessee Law is clear. If you are injured on the job, you are entitled to compensation for your injury and the right to health care and future medical treatment.

II

Assault Defense

Simple, Aggravated, Domestic, Rape, Child Abuse, Child Neglect, Disorderly Conduct

III

Robbery/Burglary Defense

Aggravated, Especially Aggravated, Armed, Shoplifting, Theft

IV

Drug Arrests & Possession of a Controlled Substance

Possession with Intent of Resale, Drug Paraphernalia, School Zone, Prescription Fraud, Conspiracy, Trafficking

V

Drunk Driving / Motor Vehicle Arrests

DUI, Implied Consent, Vehicle Misdemeanor Citations

Other Criminal Defense

Liability of Property Owners

VIII

Truck Accidents

If you have been injured or a loved one has been killed in an auto accident, you need a dedicated attorney on your side. The experienced auto accident lawyers at The WALWYN AT LAW Firm have a proven record of helping victims of injury get maximum compensation.

IX

Civil Litigation

Disagreements happen, and if the parties to a dispute cannot reach a mutually agreeable solution, then they may have to turn to the courts for help resolving their disagreement.
