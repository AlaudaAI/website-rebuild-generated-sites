Lloyd Walwyn

Orthopedic Surgeon, Licensed Attorney

Knowledge: Lloyd Walwyn is an orthopedic surgeon of over 40 years who has the distinction of having earned a law degree along the way. He has provided medico-legal expertise to the firm since 2003 and has been an integral part of the success of Walwyn & Walwyn’s personal injury practice. He has performed thousands of complex surgeries including traumatic injuries caused by accidents involving automobiles, trucks, motorcycles, atv’s, nursing home negligence and worker’s compensation injuries. He puts his legal and medical knowledge to work for Walwyn & Walwyn

Expert Witness: Lloyd Walwyn has served as an expert witness and as a doctor for independent medical exams involving all types of injuries including work injuries. On every complex personal injury case, Lloyd Walwyn has provided clients of Walwyn & Walwyn with detailed case review and analysis. He is highly qualified to provide in-house expert opinions about the value of a case and is able to increase the settlement value of a case through his understanding of traumatic injuries, severe fractures, and surgical procedures. If you are unable to think of any other lawyers in Nashville who are orthopedic and trauma specialists, don’t worry. Neither can we.
