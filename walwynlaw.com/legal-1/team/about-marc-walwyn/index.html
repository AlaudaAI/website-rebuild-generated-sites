My Philosophy:

“I WILL FIGHT TO PROTECT YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS UNDER THE LAW.”

Experienced Attorney and Former Judge!

Mr. Walwyn has won several million dollars in compensation for his clients through successful trial court work and tough negotiations. His experience on both sides of the fight make him especially knowledgeable in the tactics of the opponent and the value of a case.

Each case is different and requires experience, training, and determination in order to win in court or obtain the highest settlement amount. Marc Walwyn has proven that he has everything it takes to fight for his clients and get the money they deserve.

Marc Walwyn specializes in the areas of personal injury, wrongful death, worker’s compensation, truck accidents, slip and falls, motorcycle accidents, nursing home abuse and employment disputes. There is never a fee for a consultation and Mr. Walwyn’s team is ready to serve you.

Insurance companies have attorneys working for them to make sure they pay little or nothing for accidents caused by their customers. You should never settle an accident claim or accept money before speaking to Attorney Marc Walwyn. Know your rights. Know the value of your case. You only get one chance to get compensation for an injury or death. Maximize the your settlement or jury verdict with Marc Walwyn.
