Auto/Truck Accidents

At Walwyn & Walwyn we will leave no stone unturned when it comes to representing your interests in Vehicle Accidents. Car accidents and Truck accidents in particular are complex areas of the law and require attorneys who know how to investigate, develop and win the maximum amount of money damages for a client.

Here's what we do in every case:

Identify each and every potential defendant starting from:

  • Driver
  • Truck Owner
  • Lessee of Truck
  • Manufacturer, designer, or distributor of defective truck and trailer components
  • Police Agencies
  • Repair shops/mechanics

Research your case to provide you with Potential Witnesses such as:

  • Parties
  • Police Investigators
  • Truck Safety Experts
  • Safety Directors
  • Accident Reconstruction Experts
  • Physicians
  • Truck Drivers
  • Mechanics
  • Part Suppliers
  • Wrecker Drivers
  • Photographers
  • CB (Citizens Band) Radio Operators
  • Eyewitnesses

Collect all Potential Documents you will require:

  • National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) Records of Investigation of Accident
  • Accident Investigation Report by State Police
  • Accident Investigation Report by State Trucking Regulatory Agency
  • Accident Investigation Reports filed by Truck Owner/Operator with NTSB and State Regulatory Agency
  • Accident Investigation Reports by Owner/Operator for routine internal use
  • Insurance Policies
  • Photographs
  • Driver's Logs
  • Driver's Medical Records
  • Driver's License
  • Driver's Driving Record
  • Driver's Qualification File
  • Trip Receipts
  • Trip Invoices
  • Trip Bills of Lading
  • Repair Records
  • Parts Requisitions
  • Company Manuals
  • Financial Records for each party defendant
  • Tachometer Records
  • Driver's Cell Phone Records
  • On-board Computer Records
  • Dispatch Records
  • Fax Transmissions
  • Mobile Radio Records
  • Truck Operating Costs Records
  • Truck Configuration Records
  • Truck Licenses
  • Operating Manual for Radar Detectors
  • Operating Manual for Speed Governors
  • Wrecker Records
  • Records
  • Plaintiff's Medical Records
  • Statistics concerning Truck Operating Safety
  • Motor Carrier's Safety Rating from the Federal Highway Administration
