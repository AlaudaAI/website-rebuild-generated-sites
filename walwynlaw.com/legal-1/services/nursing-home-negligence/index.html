Nursing Home Negligence

Caring For The Elderly and others who are unable to help themselves is a very heavy responsibility. For those who find themselves in a nursing home or adult care facility, they are protected by Tennessee Laws and medical regulations.

Too Many Injuries Occur injuries while being cared for in Nursing Homes. This is because staffs fail to notice bed sores, don’t remember to turn a patient, give the wrong medications, drop a patient, or ignore other warning signs of injuries or even death.

Marc Walwyn Understands Nursing Home cases, nursing home abuse and Nursing Home negligence and has won several large settlement awards for the families of patients who have died or been injured in Nursing Homes.

Typical injuries

Typical injuries include the following:

  • Broken Bones
  • Sexual Abuse
  • Bed Sores
  • Dehydration
  • Death From Falling
  • Wrong Medications
