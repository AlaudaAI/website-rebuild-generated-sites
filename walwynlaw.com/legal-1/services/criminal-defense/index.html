Criminal Defense

Paul has handled thousands of cases and has a nose for finding the proof or the facts upon which a case turns. Sometimes only a witness or a small piece of evidence makes the difference between a finding of guilt or innocence. Therefore you can't just trust any lawyer to represent you in a criminal case. You need Paul Walwyn. He knows Tennessee criminal courts and he knows the law.

Workplace Injuries / Worker's Compensation

Tennessee Law is clear. If you are injured on the job, you are entitled to compensation for your injury and the right to health care and future medical treatment.

Truck Accidents

If you have been injured or a loved one has been killed in an auto accident, you need a dedicated attorney on your side. The experienced auto accident lawyers at The WALWYN AT LAW Firm have a proven record of helping victims of injury get maximum compensation.

Liability of Property Owners

If you operate an existing business or plan to launch a new one, your success may depend on obtaining the right legal advice.

Civil Litigation
