Workplace Injuries and Worker's Compensation

Tennessee Law is clear. If you are injured on the job, you are entitled to compensation for your injury and the right to health care and future medical treatment.

Typical Work Injuries include:

  • Injuries From Falling
  • Repetitive Motion Injuries
  • Falling Object Injury
  • Carpel Tunnel Syndrome
  • Automobile Crash
  • On Job Vehicle Injuries On Job
  • Loss Of Finger
  • Loss Of Hand
  • Loss of Leg
  • Eye Injuries
  • Closed Head Injuries
  • Burns
  • Lung Damage
  • Chemical Poisoning

Workers Are Entitled to pick a doctor from a list of providers selected by your employer. That doctor will treat you for your injuries and determine whether you have been permanently disabled to some degree. If so, you will be entitled to money damages based on the disability rating and your wages.

Worker's compensation is complicated, requires an experienced lawyer and a law firm with medical expertise. With an experienced attorney like Marc Walwyn and skillful orthopedic surgeon like Lloyd Walwyn, you can rest assured that your case will be properly evaluated and that you will receive the maximum worker's compensation award.
